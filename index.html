The HVHR Protocol

Every Layer Exists
to Prevent Theft.

Verified driver credentials, trailer inspection, load documentation, and a release approval. Nothing moves until every layer clears.

Driver Verification
License, CDL number, cell, truck & trailer plates cross-checked before loading begins
Trailer Inspection
Watertight, solid flooring confirmed prior to load. No exceptions.
Photo Documentation
Driver IDs, drivers with truck, drivers with trailer, and sealed trailer all photographed at load
Coordinated Release
The truck departs only after the BOL and photo package are verified and release is approved

FAQ

Common
Questions.

What does it mean that Ascend is a Landstar agent?

Ascend Transportation is an independently owned agency. We quote and manage your freight, and loads are booked through Landstar System and moved by its network of independent owner-operators and approved carriers. Ascend is not Landstar corporate and does not own trucks or trailers.

Who actually hauls my freight?

A Landstar owner-operator (an independent contractor leased to Landstar) or a Landstar-approved carrier, chosen for your lane and equipment. High-value loads go through our verification protocol before release.

How quickly will I get a quote?

Within one business day of a complete request. Including origin, destination, equipment, pickup date, and weight helps us price it faster.

Where do you operate?

We're based in Silicon Valley and arrange freight nationwide across the U.S., plus cross-border shipments to and from Mexico and Canada.

What should I have ready when I request a quote?

Origin and destination, pickup date, equipment type, weight and dimensions, commodity, approximate cargo value, and any security or appointment requirements.
