The HVHR Protocol

Every Layer Exists
to Prevent Theft.

Our HVHR process coordinates verified driver credentials, trailer inspection, load documentation, and a multi-party release approval — nothing moves until every layer clears.

Driver Verification
License, CDL number, cell, truck & trailer plates cross-checked before loading begins
Trailer Inspection
Watertight, solid flooring confirmed prior to load. No exceptions.
Photo Documentation
Driver IDs, drivers with truck, drivers with trailer, and sealed trailer all photographed at load
Coordinated Release
Truck cannot depart until Landstar rep approves release via verified BOL and photo package
Cargo Types
Electronics, pharmaceuticals, luxury goods, sensitive materials, high-value manufacturing
Coverage
Nationwide + Cross-Border Mexico & Canada. 24/7 agent availability.
